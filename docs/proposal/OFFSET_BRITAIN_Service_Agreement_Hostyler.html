1. Purpose

Hostyler Group agrees to design, develop, configure, deploy, and hand over a production-ready Online Competitions platform for OFFSET BRITAIN LTD, built on the Elite Premium Raffle Framework. This agreement is issued on Hostyler's standard commercial terms as our written commitment to the client. It does not require a countersignature from the client to be valid as Hostyler's offer and project record.

2. What Hostyler will build, design, develop & provide

2.1 Design & customer experience

  • Branded public website for Online Competitions (layout, homepage sections, competition catalogue, winners, legal and responsible play pages)
  • Checkout and account flows designed for clarity, trust, and mobile use
  • Staff admin interface configured for day-to-day operations (not a separate custom design project unless agreed in writing)

2.2 Development & platform

  • Full stack delivery: customer website, staff admin, central API, background jobs, and integrations described in the companion Platform Proposal & Scope of Work document (feature list version 1.5 or later as supplied with this agreement)
  • Competitions, cart, skill-based entry, instant wins (site credit, cash, physical), bonus milestones, two wallets, referrals, draw studio, compliance engine, marketing stack (GA4, Meta Pixel, CAPI, Klaviyo), email templates, and AI integration (admin insights and subtle front-end personalization)
  • Card payments via Cashflows; wallet checkout; production security (HTTPS, rate limits, monitoring hooks)
  • Self-hosted MongoDB, Redis, and object storage on infrastructure agreed with the client

2.3 Deployment & handover

  • Production deployment with HTTPS, scheduled tasks, and go-live support
  • Full database and all application files on the client's infrastructure
  • Staff admin accounts, operational handover notes, and cron configuration guidance
  • Hostyler.com client account for tickets, invoices, and downloads

2.4 Explicitly not in this build fee

  • Ongoing VPS, domain, email SMTP, Cashflows, Klaviyo, Meta, Google, or other third-party subscription fees (paid by the client to those providers)
  • Merchandise shop module (optional future add-on at no extra Hostyler fee if ever requested, as stated in the scope document)
  • Custom features outside the agreed scope document unless quoted separately in writing

3. Delivery timeframe

Hostyler will deliver the agreed scope within 10 business days from project start. Project start is the date Hostyler confirms that payment of the agreed fee (£3,360.00) has been received in full. Hostyler still needs reasonable access to deploy (hosting or VPS access, domain or staging URL, and Cashflows sandbox or live credentials when card checkout go-live is in scope); delays caused by missing access after payment are handled as in Section 6.

Delivery means: production or agreed staging environment running the scoped applications, handover completed, and the client able to operate competitions with staff admin access.

5. Technical support (12 months included)

From the go-live date (or written handover date if go-live is deferred by the client), Hostyler provides 12 months of technical support at no additional charge.

  • Support covers the delivered platform: bugs, outages caused by our code or deployment we performed, configuration questions, and reasonable guidance on admin use
  • Contact via the Hostyler.com support account and support@hostyler.com during Hostyler business hours (UK)
  • Response targets: critical production-down issues prioritized; other tickets handled in order of severity
  • Not included in free support: major new features, large-scale redesigns, third-party outages, client-side hosting misuse, or work caused by client or third-party changes outside Hostyler's control
  • Minor design and layout adjustments (copy, spacing, small UI tweaks, and similar) are included during the 12-month support period when they do not amount to a full redesign or new product scope

After 12 months, support may continue under a separate support plan if offered by Hostyler at that time.

6. Client cooperation

Timely delivery depends on reasonable cooperation: domain or DNS when ready, VPS or hosting access, Cashflows merchant setup, and marketing keys (Pixel, GA4, Klaviyo) when those integrations are part of go-live. Delays caused by missing access or approvals extend the calendar proportionally and do not reduce the scope Hostyler owes once access is complete.

7. Intellectual property & data

On full payment of the agreed fee, the client receives the deployed application and database on their infrastructure as described in Section 2.3. Framework licensing and ownership terms follow Hostyler's standard Elite Premium Raffle Framework licence supplied with handover. Customer data in the database belongs to the client.

If this agreement and the scope document differ, Hostyler will honour the more specific feature list in the scope document for product inclusions, and this agreement for price, delivery time, and support.
