1. Agreement summary

This document lists everything included in the Elite Premium Raffle Framework build for OFFSET BRITAIN LTD: a white-label online competitions business with skill-based entry, staff back office, self-hosted data, and production marketing tools. Commercial fee and payment schedule are agreed separately; this PDF is the feature contract.

2. What you get (applications included)

  • Main customer website - competitions, cart, checkout, account, winners, legal pages, referrals
  • Staff admin dashboard - full operations, settings, exports, draw studio
  • Central API backend - one engine powers website, admin, webhooks, and jobs
  • Self-hosted MongoDB - all customer, order, ticket, and competition data on your VPS
  • Self-hosted Redis - caching, rate limits, performance under traffic
  • Object storage - S3-compatible media for images and uploads

3. Public website - marketing & content

  • Configurable homepage sections (hero, ending soon, categories, winners, calls to action)
  • Competition catalogue and detail pages with galleries, video, countdowns, category filters
  • Featured and pin-to-top competitions on listings
  • Public winners gallery and homepage winner carousel (staff controls order and visibility)
  • FAQ, about, how-it-works, contact form (with abuse protection)
  • Legal pages: terms, privacy, cookies, responsible play
  • Free postal entry page with address from compliance settings
  • Referral link landing pages (/r/code)
  • Public ticket entry lookup and per-competition entry views
  • SEO per page plus site-wide SEO defaults (titles, descriptions, Open Graph, referral share images)
  • Cookie consent banner and marketing consent on accounts
  • Umami product analytics (optional env configuration)

4. Public website - cart, checkout & payments

  • Multi-competition shopping cart with live availability
  • Skill-based question gate before purchase (legal compliance)
  • Quick-add ticket quantity buttons (admin-defined presets)
  • Volume discount tiers by cart value (named tiers, min/max, percentage off)
  • Promo / discount codes at checkout
  • Referral codes and referral discounts on qualifying orders
  • Apply referral reward tickets where configured
  • Guest checkout when compliance allows; guest-to-account merge
  • Payment methods (admin-enabled): card via Cashflows, wallet-only / zero-balance checkout
  • Pay with card, site credit wallet, cash wallet, or any combination
  • Instant win reveal modal at checkout when a ticket triggers a hidden prize
  • Checkout success and failure flows with clear error messaging
  • Order confirmation emails with ticket numbers
  • Cloudflare Turnstile on auth forms (bot protection)

5. Customer accounts & dashboard

  • Sign up, login, email verification, forgot / reset password, magic link login
  • Google social sign-in; optional anonymous browse sessions
  • Profile with avatar upload, contact details, addresses
  • My orders, my tickets / entries, my wins (main draw, instant win, bonus prizes)
  • Site credit wallet and cash wallet balances with transaction history
  • Cash wallet top-up and withdrawal requests (per rules)
  • Referral hub: personal code, share link, tier progress, stats, leaderboard-style metrics
  • Responsible play: monthly spend limits, self-exclusion, request to lift exclusion (admin review)
  • Marketing preferences linked to Klaviyo when enabled
  • Web push notification opt-in and preferences (when campaigns enabled)

6. Competition management (admin)

  • Full competition builder: title, slug, pricing, schedules, draw date, status workflow (draft, active, paused, ended, pending draw, drawn, cancelled)
  • Ticket limits: minimum before draw, maximum pool, max per user, holds and sold counts
  • Main prize details, prize cost for accounting, cash alternative for winners
  • Private, community, featured, hero / appear-first flags; cash-only competitions; referral-reward competitions
  • Categories (multi-select), rich HTML descriptions, main image, gallery, competition video
  • Landing video upload with frame extraction for previews
  • Skill question per competition or import from global Q&A library
  • Duplicate competition with instant prizes, discounts, and media
  • Soft delete / restore competitions
  • End-draw workflow and undo draw with reason history
  • Scheduled job moves competitions toward draw when due

7. Instant win system

Hidden prizes on specific ticket numbers. Wins trigger at checkout before the main draw.

7.1 Prize types (included for OFFSET BRITAIN LTD)

TypeWhat happens
Site creditCredited instantly to Site Credit Wallet
CashCash prize recorded; payout via admin / cash wallet processes
Physical prizeFulfilment by staff; shipping and winner comms
  • Reusable instant prize templates (library) and per-competition assignments
  • Prize name, value, image, winning ticket numbers (manual or auto-generated)
  • Instant win emails; claimed / unclaimed fulfilment queue in admin
  • Instant win analytics, filters, CSV export
  • Option to block credit-card checkout when instant wins are in cart (compliance setting)

  • Public progress display on competition pages
  • Automatic milestone firing plus admin retrigger for pending fires
  • Bonus win fulfilment queue and customer notification emails

18. Security, performance & operations

  • HTTPS, secure session cookies, separate admin origin recommended
  • Rate limits on auth, payments, contact (Redis-backed when enabled)
  • Content Security Policy for payments, analytics, Turnstile, social scripts
  • Redis cache for catalogue and settings; live tickets and wallets never stale-cached
  • Background jobs (cron): check draws due, bonus milestones, abandoned order cleanup, scheduled notifications, ticketing anomaly checks
  • Sentry error monitoring in production
  • Health checks for database and Redis; build version endpoint
  • Idempotent checkout and webhook handling; order fulfilment engine with ticket allocation

19. AI integration

A smart system built into the platform that learns from your live data and helps you run competitions with less guesswork.

19.1 Admin and operations

  • Analyzes users, visit patterns, behaviour, spending, basket habits, competition performance, and referral activity
  • Surfaces trends and alerts so staff see what is working and what is slowing down
  • Suggests what to upload next (competitions, prizes, creatives, promos) based on your audience patterns
  • Recommends timing, pricing bands, and catalogue mix aligned with historical demand on your site

19.2 Customer website (same AI engine)

  • The same AI layer runs on the public site to personalize layout, ordering, and highlights in a subtle way
  • Influences discovery and engagement indirectly (for example which competitions appear first, relevant nudges, and content emphasis) without aggressive pop-ups or hard selling
  • Respects compliance, consent, and responsible play settings at all times
